PhD · Dynamics & Control

Friction is the disturbance.
Understanding it is the control problem.

My doctoral work at the University of Tehran studies sliding and pre-sliding friction in Lorentz / voice-coil actuator systems. The research combines physical structure with data-driven learning for better estimation and compensation in precision motion.
